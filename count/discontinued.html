Discontinue a product

Search any product. Pending Discontinuation if till stock is above 0, Discontinued when it reaches 0.
Discontinued products are left out of stock counts, date checks, order baskets and supplier orders, out of stock and low stock lists, wholesale price compare lists, active sales lists and new Rewards promotions. Pending products are still counted and date checked, but never reordered. Past sales, invoices, accounts and profit reports are kept. Nothing is deleted and the till is not changed by this page.
